## Project analysis

### Project analysis, User profile
I made this website for a recruiter who looks at student CVs. They just want to see quickly who I am, what I'm studying, what I know and how to contact me.

### Project analysis, Information architecture
I made it as a single page. I split it into sections: Hero, About, Skills, Education and Contact. I put the Hero first so people see my name and that I'm a Computer Engineering student. Then About to say a bit about me and that I'm looking for an internship. Next is Skills to show the languages I know. Then Education where I put my degree first. At the end is Contact so they can see my email and GitHub. I used a simple menu at the top to go to each section. I used links so when you click it goes down to that part. I tried to keep the order simple so it's easy to scan. The sections are one after another with space between them. Everything is on one page so you don't need to go to other pages. I kept it short so someone can look at it quickly without getting lost. I wanted the most important things first and less important at the end.

### Project analysis, Visual design
I used a dark background because I think it's nice and easy to read. I used blue for the links and buttons to make them stand out a bit. I used the Inter font from Google Fonts because it's clear and easy to see. I left some space between sections so it doesn't look too full. On mobile it stacks one below the other so it's still easy to read. I just used basic colors and borders to make it look clean. I didn't add anything extra, I just tried to keep it simple so the content is what matters. I made sure there is enough contrast between text and background to read it well. The photo is on the side on bigger screens and below on smaller ones. I wanted it to look neat without being too decorated.
